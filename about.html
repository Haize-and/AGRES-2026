About The Faculty

Nurturing Excellence in Agricultural Sciences Since 1999

A Tradition of Innovation

The Faculty of Agriculture at Wayamba University of Sri Lanka stands as a beacon of agricultural education and research. Located in the heart of the agrarian landscape in Makandura, we are dedicated to advancing the frontiers of agricultural science through rigorous academic programs and impactful research.

Our symposium, AGRES, provides a vital platform for our undergraduates to present their findings, fostering a culture of scientific inquiry and critical thinking essential for future leaders in the sector.

Global Impact

Research addressing international challenges.

Community Focus

Solutions for local farming communities.

Our Mission

To produce high-quality graduates with knowledge, skills, and attitudes to meet the challenges in the agriculture sector through teaching, research, and outreach activities for national development.

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in agriculture education and research, contributing to sustainable development and food security locally and globally.
